WeeLend Cooperative Information

⚖️ Membership & Responsibility

By joining WeeLend, you agree to meet your share commitments and repay any loan, MLF Easy Installment, or Marimar's Credit Cart purchase in full, on schedule.

Philippine law does not imprison anyone for failing to pay a debt (1987 Constitution, Art. III, Sec. 20) — this is a civil, not a criminal, matter. However, agreeing to become a WeeLend member or borrower means you accept real consequences for not honoring that agreement:

  • Your outstanding balance remains a debt owed to the cooperative and its members — it does not disappear.
  • If you leave the cooperative with an unpaid loan, your share contributions are applied against that balance first; anything beyond that stays on record as a receivable owed by you.
  • Your credit limit, future loan eligibility, and dividend standing are all directly affected by your payment history.
  • The cooperative may pursue any lawful civil remedy available to recover what's owed.

This section is a plain-language summary, not a substitute for a formal member agreement reviewed by legal counsel.

👥 Membership & User Roles

Admin – Full control over approvals, loans, fees, and profiles.

Member – Approved by Admin. Contributes monthly shares, may act as a guarantor, and shares in the cooperative's dividends.

Borrower – Approved by Admin. Can request loans and use MLF Easy Installment / Marimar's Credit Cart / Pa-benta, but does not contribute shares or receive dividends.

Security Guarantor – A separate, lightweight account for someone named as a required backup contact on a loan or purchase over ₱10,000. Not a coop member — just verifies their identity so they're a reachable, real person on file.

💰 Capital Contribution (Shares)
  • Share value: ₱1,000 per share
  • New members start at ₱1,000/month; Admin may adjust a member's monthly commitment afterward.
  • Shares must be paid monthly to remain in good standing — your Paid Months and Total Shares are tracked on your dashboard.
  • 📅 Planned: all active members' monthly share commitment will increase by ₱1,000 starting next fiscal year (not yet in effect).
🪪 Identity Verification
  • A one-time selfie + selfie-with-ID + government ID upload, submitted from Edit Profile.
  • Required — and must be admin-approved — before you can submit a coop loan, MLF Easy Installment, Marimar's Credit Cart, or Pa-benta request.
  • Your Emergency Contact info is required as well; both are reused across every request you make, so you only complete them once.
📄 Loan, Easy Installment & Credit Cart Terms
  • Coop loan interest: 10% per month
  • MLF Easy Installment & Marimar's Credit Cart: 10% flat interest over the term, no downpayment needed for Credit Cart
  • Payment schedule: Monthly or Semi-Monthly (every 15 days)
  • Late fee: 3% immediately after the due date, escalating to 5% after 15 days unpaid
  • Only Admin may waive a late fee
  • A Security Guarantor (verified third-party contact) is required for any request over ₱10,000 — you cannot name yourself as your own Security Guarantor
🤝 Guarantor & Effort System

WeeLend has two separate guarantor mechanisms — don't confuse them:

  • Effort-Pool Guarantor (coop loans) – you may name yourself; whoever is named earns Effort credit toward that guarantor's own share of the year-end payout.
  • Security Guarantor (any request over ₱10,000) – must be someone else, a real reachable contact who completes their own identity verification. This protects the cooperative if a borrower can't be reached.
🏁 Fiscal Year & Dividend Distribution
  • Every payment collected splits: 10% to the Trust Fund, then 70% of what's left to the Capital Pool, 30% to the Effort Pool. Late fees go 100% to the Trust Fund.
  • Capital Pool is shared among active members in proportion to their monthly share commitment.
  • Effort Pool is shared among active members in proportion to their guaranteed (effort-pool) loan volume.
  • If a member leaves the cooperative with an unpaid loan balance, that balance is tracked as an uncollected receivable and excluded from dividend calculations until it's actually collected — so payouts always reflect real cash on hand, not money still owed.
🛠 Admin Operational Guidelines
  • Approve all registrations and identity verifications
  • Monitor available funds and collections regularly
  • Digital receipts serve as official records

📜 Cooperative Loan Agreement — Terms and Conditions

This is a binding agreement between you ("the Borrower") and WEE LENDING COOPERATIVE ("the Cooperative"). By checking "I Agree" and submitting this loan request, you are entering into this agreement, in accordance with Republic Act No. 9520 (the Philippine Cooperative Code of 2008) and the Cooperative's by-laws.

1. Loan and Interest

You are requesting the loan amount, term, and payment schedule shown above. Interest accrues at 10% per month on the outstanding principal, on a simple (non-compounding) basis, for the full agreed term.

2. Repayment

You agree to repay the loan in full — principal plus interest — according to the payment schedule shown above, without need of further demand from the Cooperative.

3. Late Payment

Any installment not paid on its due date is subject to a late fee of 3% of the overdue amount. If an installment remains unpaid for more than 15 days past its due date, the late fee increases to 5%.

4. If You Do Not Pay

Philippine law does not imprison anyone for failing to pay a debt (1987 Constitution, Art. III, Sec. 20) — this is a civil, not a criminal, matter. Still, non-payment has real, binding consequences:

  • Your outstanding balance remains a debt owed to the Cooperative and its members. It does not disappear, is not forgiven, and continues to accrue as described above until paid.
  • Your share capital contributions, if any, may be applied against any unpaid balance before any other use.
  • Missed or incomplete payments directly and immediately reduce your future credit limit and loan eligibility with the Cooperative.
  • If you leave the Cooperative with an unpaid balance, that balance remains collectible and is not extinguished by your departure.
  • The Cooperative may pursue any lawful civil remedy available to it to recover what is owed, including but not limited to formal demand, offset against your share capital, referral to a collection process, or civil action.
5. Your Consent and Data

By submitting this request, you consent to the Cooperative's collection, verification, and use of the personal and identification information you have provided, for purposes of loan processing, credit evaluation, and enforcement of this agreement, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

6. How You Agree

Your acceptance is given electronically, by checking the box below and submitting this request. Under the Electronic Commerce Act of 2000 (Republic Act No. 8792), this electronic acceptance has the same legal effect, validity, and enforceability as a signature on paper. The exact terms shown to you at the moment of acceptance are recorded and kept on file as proof of this agreement.

7. Severability

If any provision of this agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the rest of the agreement remains in full force and effect.

8. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, including the Philippine Cooperative Code of 2008 (RA 9520) and the Civil Code of the Philippines, and by the Cooperative's by-laws and duly approved policies.
