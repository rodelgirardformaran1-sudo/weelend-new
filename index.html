WeeLend Cooperative Information

👥 Membership & User Roles

Admin – First registered user. Full control over approvals, loans, fees, and profiles.

Member – Approved by Admin. Provides shares, may act as guarantor, and monitors borrowers.

Borrower – Approved by Admin. Can only view and manage personal loan information.

💰 Capital Contribution (Shares)
  • Share value: ₱1,000 per share
  • New Member limit: 1 share (₱1,000)
  • Returning Member limit: 2 shares (₱2,000)
  • Admin limit: 5 shares (₱5,000)
  • Shares must be paid monthly to remain in good standing.
  • Shares will increase (₱1,000) for the next fiscal year
📄 Loan Terms & Conditions
  • Loan amount: ₱1,000 – ₱100,000
  • Interest: 10% per month
  • Payment: Monthly or Semi-Monthly (every 15 days)
  • Late fee: 3% immediately, escalates to 5% after 15 days
  • Only Admin may waive penalties
  • All payments generate digital receipts
🤝 Guarantee & Effort System
  • All loans must have a guarantor
  • Members/Admins may self-guarantee
  • Guaranteeing borrowers earns Effort
  • Effort affects year-end profit payout
  • Guarantors must monitor borrower payments
🏁 Fiscal Year & Payout Distribution
  • One-year fiscal cycle
  • Initial share capital returned in full
  • 70% → Capital Pool (shared by shareholders)
  • 30% → Effort Pool (based on guaranteed borrowers)
🛠 Admin Operational Guidelines
  • Monitor cash-on-hand daily
  • Approve all registrations
  • Reset button used only at fiscal year end
  • Digital receipts serve as official records
🌱 Vision

To create a community where borrowers become future lenders, building capital, discipline, and opportunity for others.

🎯 Mission
  • Empower members through accessible credit
  • Promote accountability via guarantor system
  • Ensure fair and sustainable profit sharing
  • Educate users for financial independence
💙 Core Values (The WeeLend Way)
  • Integrity – Transparent tracking and receipts
  • Growth – One-year financial discipline cycle
  • Support – Compassion during hardship
📜 By-Laws Summary
  • Membership approved by Admin
  • Shares fund the lending pool
  • All loans require guarantors
  • Profit distributed yearly
  • Admin may amend policies

Borrower Code of Conduct & Rules

Welcome to WeeLend. By applying for a loan, you are partnering with members who pooled their savings to support your financial needs. Follow these rules to build trust and qualify for future opportunities.

📝 Eligibility & Registration
  • Register using a valid email address.
  • Status remains Pending until Admin approval.
  • Government ID upload is required.
  • Terms & Conditions must be accepted before loan processing.
🤝 The Guarantor System
  • Every loan must have a Member or Admin guarantor.
  • Your guarantor monitors your repayment progress.
  • Missed payments affect your guarantor’s Effort record.
  • Maintain good communication with your guarantor.
💳 Loan Terms & Interest
  • Loan range: ₱1,000 – ₱100,000
  • Interest rate: 10% per month
  • Payment options:
    • Once monthly (30th)
    • Semi-monthly (15th & 30th)
  • Borrowers must track due dates via dashboard.
⚠️ Penalties & Late Fees
  • 3% penalty applied immediately after due date.
  • Penalty increases to 5% after 15 days unpaid.
  • Only Admin may approve late fee waivers.
  • Waivers depend on payment history and hardship review.
🧾 Payments & Digital Receipts
  • All payments generate official digital receipts.
  • Receipts include:
    • Principal
    • Interest
    • Late fees (if any)
  • Keep receipts for your personal records.
🚀 Path to Becoming a Lender
  • Consistent on-time payments improve eligibility.
  • Good standing borrowers may be prioritized for membership.
  • Members earn from Capital and Effort pools.
❌ Grounds for Disqualification
  • False registration information
  • Repeated missed payments without communication
  • Fraudulent activity within the app
